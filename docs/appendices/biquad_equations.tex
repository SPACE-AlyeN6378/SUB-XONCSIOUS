\section{Biquad Coefficient Equations}
\label{app:biquad_equations}

The following equations are extracted from Audio EQ Cookbook by Robert-Bristow Johnson,
which are used to calculate the coefficients of the second-order infinite impulse response (IIR) biquad filter 
(See \Cref{eq:biquad_transfer} for the transfer function). The Quality Factor ($Q$) is used to
control resonance in the centre frequency ($f_0$) \cite{cookbook}. Please note that bandwidth ($BW$) is not used 
in the implementation.

\subsection{Common Variables}

The following variables are used throughout the filter coefficient calculations:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bq_omega}
    \omega_0 = \frac{2\pi f_0}{f_s}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
    A = 10^{\frac{\mathrm{dB}_{\mathrm{gain}}}{40}}
\end{equation}

For filters that use the quality factor,
\begin{equation}
    \alpha = \frac{\sin(\omega_0)}{2Q}
\end{equation}

For shelving filters, the shelf slope parameter ($S$) is used:
\begin{equation}
    \alpha =
\frac{\sin(\omega_0)}{2}
\sqrt{\left(A+\frac{1}{A}\right)\left(\frac{1}{S}-1\right)+2}
\end{equation}

Where,
\begin{itemize}
    \item $f_0$ is the cutoff or centre frequency (Hz),
    \item $f_s$ is the sampling frequency/sample rate (Hz),
    \item $Q$ is the quality factor,
    \item $A$ is the linear gain corresponding to the specified gain in decibels,
    \item $S$ is the shelf slope parameter.
\end{itemize}

After computing the coefficients in the next sections, all filters are normalized by 
dividing every coefficient by $a_0$:

$$
    b_0 \leftarrow \frac{b_0}{a_0},\qquad
    b_1 \leftarrow \frac{b_1}{a_0},\qquad
    b_2 \leftarrow \frac{b_2}{a_0}
$$

$$
    a_1 \leftarrow \frac{a_1}{a_0}, \qquad
    a_2 \leftarrow \frac{a_2}{a_0}
$$

Then the transfer function would look like this:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:norm_biquad_transfer}
    H(z) = \frac{\left(\frac{b_0}{a_0}\right) 
    + \left(\frac{b_1}{a_0}\right) z^{-1} 
    + \left(\frac{b_2}{a_0}\right) z^{-2}}
    {1 + \left(\frac{a_1}{a_0}\right) z^{-1} 
    + \left(\frac{a_2}{a_0}\right) z^{-2}}
\end{equation}

The normalized denominator coefficient becomes $a_0 = 1$, so that the program
can only work with 5 coefficients instead of 6.

\subsection{Low-Pass Filter}

$$
b_0=\frac{1-\cos\omega_0}{2},\qquad
b_1=1-\cos\omega_0, \qquad
b_2=\frac{1-\cos\omega_0}{2}
$$

$$
a_0=1+\alpha,\qquad
a_1=-2\cos\omega_0, \qquad
a_2=1-\alpha
$$

\subsection{Band-Pass Filter}
$$
a_0=1+\alpha, \qquad  a_1=-2\cos\omega_0, \qquad, a_2=1-\alpha
$$

For Constant Peak Gain (\SI{0}{\decibel} peak gain), 
$$
b_0=\alpha, \qquad  b_1=0, \qquad, b_2=-\alpha
$$

For Constant Skirt Gain (Peak gain $= Q$), 
$$
b_0=Q\alpha, \qquad  b_1=0, \qquad, b_2=-Q\alpha
$$

\subsection{Peaking Equalizer}
$$
b_0=1+\alpha A, \qquad b_1=-2\cos\omega_0, \qquad b_2=1-\alpha A
$$

$$
a_0=1+\frac{\alpha}{A}, \qquad a_1=-2\cos\omega_0, \qquad a_2=1-\frac{\alpha}{A}

\subsection{Low-Shelf Filter}
$$
b_0=A\left[(A+1)-(A-1)\cos\omega_0+2\sqrt{A}\alpha\right], \qquad
b_1=2A\left[(A-1)-(A+1)\cos\omega_0\right], \qquad
$$

$$
b_2=A\left[(A+1)-(A-1)\cos\omega_0-2\sqrt{A}\alpha\right]
$$

$$
a_0=(A+1)+(A-1)\cos\omega_0+2\sqrt{A}\alpha, \qquad a_1=-2\left[(A-1)+(A+1)\cos\omega_0\right]
$$

$$
a_2=(A+1)+(A-1)\cos\omega_0-2\sqrt{A}\alpha
$$

\subsection{High-Shelf Filter}
$$
b_0=A\left[(A+1)+(A-1)\cos\omega_0+2\sqrt{A}\alpha\right], \qquad b_1=-2A\left[(A-1)+(A+1)\cos\omega_0\right]
$$
$$
b_2=A\left[(A+1)+(A-1)\cos\omega_0-2\sqrt{A}\alpha\right]
$$

$$
a_0=(A+1)-(A-1)\cos\omega_0+2\sqrt{A}\alpha, \qquad a_1=2\left[(A-1)-(A+1)\cos\omega_0\right]

$$
