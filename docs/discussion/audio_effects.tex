\subsection{Audio Effects}

Producers apply audio effects to their track to enhance perceptual quality, 
adding depth and character to the sound. Each effect is dependent on the audio sample
being sent, whether directly or from another audio effect. Therefore, they must execute
on the same thread as the audio input, without breaching real-time constraints or any CPU overhead. 
\Cref{fig:audio_fx_pipeline} shows an example of a placement of the audio effects system within this real-time signal path, 
depending on the audio effects the producer has added (\cref{prompt:audio_fx_block_diag}).
