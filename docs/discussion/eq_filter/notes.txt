"For parametric equalization, the RBJ biquad structure is appropriate 
because EQ processing requires accurate realization of a defined second-order 
transfer function with independent control of frequency, bandwidth, and gain. 
The Audio EQ Cookbook provides coefficient equations for these common EQ responses, 
making biquads an efficient and widely adopted solution for digital equalizers."

"However, synthesizer filters have different requirements from equalizers. 
Filter cutoff frequency and resonance are often continuously modulated by 
low-frequency oscillators, envelopes, and performance controls. For these 
time-varying applications, Wishnick proposes the State Variable Filter as a 
suitable structure due to its time-varying stability, artifact-free parameter 
interpolation, and ability to generate multiple filter responses."
