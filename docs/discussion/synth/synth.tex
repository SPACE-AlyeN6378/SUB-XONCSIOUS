\subsection{Design of Synthesizer}
\label{sec:synthesizer}

Synthesizers provide flexible ways of creating artifical sounds used in many genres of music
and other entertainment industries. Producers crafts these waveforms generated by elecronic 
oscillators by applying various audio effects such as filters, unisons, Low-Frequency Oscillations
(LFO), etc. therefore creating a distinct timbre which describes the true characteristics
of a typical synth. The following subsections below show how the essential components of a synthesizer
are modelled.
